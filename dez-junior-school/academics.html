Learning by Doing

Farm Activities

Every class takes a turn in our school garden and demonstration farm, growing vegetables, tending fruit trees, and keeping poultry. It's where Science and Social Studies lessons come to life — pupils learn where food comes from, practise teamwork, and take home real responsibility.

Produce from the farm supports our school kitchen, and P.6 & P.7 pupils run a small pupil-led "Harvest Club" that tracks planting seasons and yields.

Beyond the Classroom

Clubs, sports, and enrichment for every interest

Academic strength grows alongside talent outside the classroom. Every pupil joins at least one club or team each term, from Reading Club and Science Club to Athletics, Football, Netball, and the Dez Choir.

Our P.7 pupils receive dedicated PLE preparation and mentorship, plus guidance from our Director of Studies on choosing the right secondary school for the next step in their journey.
