How it works

The Enrollment Process

1

Enquire

Call, email, or visit our front office to ask about places, fees, and boarding options for your child's class level.

2

Submit an Application

Complete the admission form and submit it with the required documents listed below.

3

Placement Assessment

New students sit a short English and Mathematics placement assessment to guide class placement.

4

Family Interview

A brief, friendly meeting with the Director of Studies to discuss your child's needs and answer your questions.

5

Offer & Enrollment

Once accepted, complete fees payment and orientation to secure your child's place for the term.

Documents Needed

Required Documents

Please bring the following when submitting your application:

  • Completed admission form
  • Copy of the student's birth certificate
  • Previous school's leaving certificate or testimonial
  • Most recent report card / academic transcript
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Immunisation record
  • Copy of a parent or guardian's national ID
