Who We Are

Building a family culture rooted in excellence.

Since 1996, Dez Junior School has grown from a single block on Dezderio Road into one of Mateete's most trusted primary schools — guided by one belief: every learner deserves a personal path to success.

Our Story

From one classroom block to a 1,240-pupil community

Dez Junior School was founded in 1996 by a group of local educators who wanted a school where academic rigour and genuine care for young learners could sit side by side. What began with three classrooms and 64 day-scholars has grown into a full boarding and day primary school taking pupils from Primary One through Primary Seven.

Today, our 12-acre campus is home to bright, well-resourced classrooms, an ICT and Innovation Room, a resource-rich library, sports grounds, and boarding houses that carry the same family culture our founders imagined.
